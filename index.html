About

Robotics isn't just about building machines that move. It's about building systems we can trust to act safely without losing what makes them useful.

My fascination with robots started early and grew from electronics, embedded systems, and sensors into motion planning, optimization, safe control, and learning.

Working in the robotics industry, I saw first-hand how safety often comes at the cost of efficiency. That tension is what brought me to a Ph.D. I now work with Professor Ryan Cosner on robots that can reason about safety, make informed decisions under uncertainty, and adapt without giving up on their tasks.
